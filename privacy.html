Privacy Policy for Lulu App

At Lulu App, available on the Google Play Store (View App), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

Information We Collect

We may collect limited information such as device details and usage analytics to improve app performance and user experience.

How We Use Your Information

Collected information is used only to enhance functionality and user experience. We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties.

User Data Deletion Instructions

If you wish to delete your account data associated with Lulu App, please send an email to support@sparkonline.in with the subject line Request for Data Deletion.

Once we receive your request, we will delete your data permanently from our systems within 7 business days.

You can also delete all local data by uninstalling the app from your device.
